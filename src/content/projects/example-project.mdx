## Math

Inline $\hat{y}_t = f_\theta(x_{t-k:t})$, or as a block:

$$
\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \left( y_i - f_\theta(x_i) \right)^2 + \lambda \lVert \theta \rVert_2^2
$$

for epoch in range(cfg.epochs):
